## FAQ

**Q: How can AI help my specific industry and business operations?**
AI can optimize processes, reduce costs, and enhance customer experiences. Applications vary by industry — e.g., personalized recommendations in retail, predictive maintenance in manufacturing, AI-driven analytics in finance.

**Q: What is your process for identifying AI opportunities?**
Start with an AI readiness assessment, identify high-impact use cases, develop a proof of concept, refine, then scale with ongoing support.

**Q: What AI technologies do you specialize in?**
Machine learning, NLP, computer vision, and automation. Best fit depends on business needs.

**Q: What are typical costs, timelines, and ROI expectations?**
Costs vary by complexity. Timelines range from weeks (small pilots) to months (full deployment). ROI often includes cost savings, efficiency gains, or revenue growth.

**Q: How do you ensure solutions are scalable, secure, and compliant?**
Best practices in AI ethics, data privacy (GDPR, HIPAA), and cybersecurity. Solutions designed to scale while maintaining compliance.

**Q: What are the first steps to explore AI?**
Start with a consultation, assess challenges and AI readiness, define a strategy, run a pilot project, gradually scale adoption.

**Q: What level of AI expertise do we need in-house?**
None. Codon handles the technical side while guiding the team through adoption. Training and support provided.
