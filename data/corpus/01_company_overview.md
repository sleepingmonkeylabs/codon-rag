## Identity

- **Full name:** Codon Consulting AB
- **Tagline:** "Making AI Happen"
- **Founded:** 2019
- **HQ:** Stockholm, Sweden
- **Website:** https://www.codon.se
- **Email:** info@codon.se
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/codon-consulting (linked from site)

Codon is a Stockholm-based AI consulting firm specializing in reliable, interpretable, and controllable AI systems that address real-world business challenges. They provide businesses with AI-driven solutions that enhance efficiency, foster innovation, and support sustainable growth.

---

## Mission & Positioning

> "We empower businesses with AI solutions that drive efficiency, innovation, and growth."

Codon positions itself as a **trusted partner** rather than a vendor. Their stated approach is to be curious, collaborative, and innovative. Since 2019 they have developed a clear, collaborative process for turning ideas into real results.

## Values & Purpose

### Innovation with a Purpose
Delivers solutions that are cutting-edge but purposeful. Combines expertise with a shared commitment to meaningful outcomes.

### Proven Track Record
Portfolio spans manufacturing process optimization, content moderation, pharmaceutical scheduling, life sciences, and more.

### AI for Good
Belief in the transformative potential of AI and dedication to unlocking it responsibly.

### Client-Centric Collaboration
Partnership model: from initial Discovery phase through ongoing Monitoring/Support.

### Global Reach, Local Values
Global perspective anchored in Stockholm, Sweden. Recognizes the importance of understanding local business landscapes.

### Responsible Business
Commitment to sustainability and responsible AI operations. Emphasizes ethics, GDPR compliance, and responsible AI frameworks.

---

## AI Innovation Process (4 Stages)

### 1. Discovery
Thorough understanding of the client's business, goals, and challenges. Collaborative workshops, feasibility assessment, opportunity identification.

### 2. Strategy
Tailored AI blueprint detailing steps, technologies, and timelines. Aligned with client objectives.

### 3. Implementation
Integration of state-of-the-art technologies. Iterative approach with continuous refinement based on feedback.

### 4. Monitoring & Support
Long-term partnership model. Monitoring, optimization, and fine-tuning of deployed solutions. Ensures ongoing value and adaptability.

## Key Partnerships

### Siemens Industrial Edge Solution Partner (certified January 2026)
Codon is a certified **Siemens Industrial Edge Solution Partner**. This enables:
- Secure, scalable gateway for AI deployment on the shop floor
- Centralized app management (deploy to one machine or one thousand)
- Industrial-grade hardware designed for factory environments
- Security by design meeting strictest industrial standards
- Bridge between device-level data (ISA-95 Level 0–2) and strategic decision-making (Level 4)

Press release quote (David Richardson, Business Developer):
> "Our strength isn't just being close to the hardware; it's being close to the decision. By joining the Siemens Industrial Edge ecosystem, we are better equipped to build the 'vertical' bridge."
