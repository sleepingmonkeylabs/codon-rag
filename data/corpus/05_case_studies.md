## Industrial & Manufacturing

### 1. Transforming Pest Control with AI-Driven IoT Sensor Analysis
**URL:** https://www.codon.se/blog/transforming-pest-control-with-al-driven-iot-sensor-analysis

**Client:** Leading pest control company
**Challenge:** Managing vast volumes of IoT sensor data generated around the clock. Manual interpretation was no longer scalable; unclear alarms made prioritization difficult — increasing costs and slowing response times.

**Solution:** Machine learning chain of models that:
- Automatically detect changes in movement patterns
- Classify activities as pertinent or non-pertinent
- Rank them to support smarter prioritization and faster decision-making

**Value delivered:**
- Uniform, objective analysis framework enabling scalable operations
- Freed up analyst time for complex cases and key clients
- Consistent efficiency handling large data volumes
- Reduced dependency on individual expertise

### 3. Increased Production Throughput by AI-Driven Scheduling
**URL:** https://www.codon.se/blog/increased-production-throughput-by-ai-driven-scheduling

**Client:** Large pharmaceutical company
**Challenge:** Increase throughput on a complex production line handling multiple products, each with specific production rules. Could not be modeled with standard linear scheduling software.

**Solution:** Using AI, mathematical optimization, and constraint programming:
- Modeled the production line including all product-specific constraints
- Developed an optimization engine paired with bespoke front- and backend solutions
- Enabled fast and flexible process scheduling

**Value delivered:**
- Faster, more accurate scheduling
- Increased throughput on the production line
- Immediate ROI
