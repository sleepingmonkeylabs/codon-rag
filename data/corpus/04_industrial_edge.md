## The Problem They Solve

The "data silo" between the shop floor and the boardroom. Moving massive volumes of sensor data to the cloud is often:
- Too slow for real-time decisions
- Too expensive for large-scale deployments
- A security risk for sensitive process data

**Codon's solution:** AI models that run **locally**, directly on the production line.

## Industry-Specific Edge Use Cases

### Manufacturing
High-frequency vibration analysis for predictive maintenance — detecting mechanical fatigue before breakdown occurs.

### Process Industries
Real-time optimization of chemical throughput and flow rates. Closed-loop control models adjust parameters instantly based on live sensor data.

### Life Sciences
Dynamic process scheduling and resource optimization. Synchronize complex lab workflows and equipment usage in real-time, reducing idle time and ensuring validated protocols.

### Property Tech
Distributed AI for smart building energy management. Real-time adjustments to HVAC and lighting systems based on occupancy patterns and ambient conditions to minimize carbon footprint and operational costs.
