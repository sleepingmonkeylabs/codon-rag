# CLASS 1 — SCRIPT

---

## 1. Chatty guy *(~10 min, no interruption)*
Let him place Vima historically. Monk/householder divide. The whole social context. He'll frame it as hierarchy. Let it sit.

---

## 2. Transition

"So. The field looks impure. Here's what the sutra does with that."

---

## 3. Reading — Chapter 1

*(Thurman translation — verify verbatim before class. Read slowly, no commentary yet.)*

Śāriputra thought: "If the bodhisattva's purification of the buddha-field results in such a pure land, why then, when Śākyamuni Buddha was practicing the bodhisattva way, did he not purify his buddha-field? Why does this buddha-field appear so impure?"

The Buddha, knowing his thought, said: "Śāriputra, what do you think — is it because the sun and moon are impure that the blind cannot see them?"

Śāriputra replied: "No, World-honored One. That is the fault of the blind, not of the sun and moon."

The Buddha then pressed his toe against the earth, and immediately the whole billion-world universe was adorned with hundreds of thousands of jewels. The assembly was filled with wonder. Then the Buddha released his toe, and the world returned to its former appearance.

---

## 4. Point 1 *(doctrinal)*

Pure doesn't mean clean. It means unedited. Śāriputra isn't seeing the world — he's seeing his filters. The buddhafield isn't an image. It's the entire field of contact. Śāriputra filters across all sense fields, not just sight. The Buddha removes the filter — and everything shifts, not just what's visible. The buddhafield was always there. The filters were in the way.

---

## 5. Point 2 *(group)*

When you react to someone's trait, you're not perceiving them. You're perceiving your own pattern firing. They just matched it. Your opinion of them is a report on your own habits.

Staying present isn't an act of will. It's catching the categorizing before it finishes.

When that lands — the pattern self-liberates. Nothing to do. When it doesn't — fallback is simple: be kind. Not as a spiritual achievement. Just as a floor.

Self-liberation when recognition is clean. Kindness when it isn't. Those are the only two options that don't add more sems on top of sems.

---

## 6. Turn it to the room *(last 10-15 min)*

"This sangha is your buddhafield right now."

Stop. Don't explain. Don't invite sharing.

Someone will try to process it into a comfortable conclusion. Let it run. It exhausts itself in 2-3 minutes if you don't feed it.

Then: "Let's just sit with this."

No instruction. No framing. Don't call it meditation. Don't call it anything.

The silence after that teaching is the teaching. Śāriputra's move is to fill the gap. Not filling it is the demonstration.
